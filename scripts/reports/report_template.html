1 Summary

Objective. Does Population Based Training (PBT), which adapts the learning rate (LR) while fine-tuning the pretrained Particle Transformer (ParT), give a lower mistag than the standard ParT training recipe with the same budget of five models and 50 epochs?

Experimental setup and metric definitions

Training samples. “800k” and “8M” name the first 800 thousand and 8 million source entries of each of the three flavour files (b, c, light). After preselection they hold 2,392,232 and 23,921,449 jets. Training (800k) is a subset of Training (8M); from 10 November the 999,999 Validation (1M) rows are removed from Training (8M).

Validation and Test. Validation (150k, until 9 November) and Validation (1M, from 10 November) select the model and drive PBT. Test (2.8M), 2,825,125 jets, is evaluated once per selected model. Test (9.0M), 8,970,477 jets, was used only in #10.

Metric. The mistag is in % (0.3018 means 0.3018%): the geometric mean of the background mistag rates at b-tag 80% and 90% (against c and light jets) and c-tag 50% and 80% (against b and light jets). Lower is better. Changes such as “1.4% better” are relative changes of the mistag.

Compute budget. Every method trains five models for 50 epochs from the same pretrained checkpoint. The probe variants (#25, #26) used 9–12% more training; the 8M reference #24 is a sixth standard ParT model.

Uncertainty. Intervals are 95%, from a block bootstrap of the Test sample. They do not include seed-to-seed variation, which the seeds show directly.

Reading the timeline. Each phase lists its primary experiments; diagnostic checks and secondary modifications are under “Supporting experiments” and open from any link to them. # is the trial number; it links to the trial's runs in the experiment log. Dates are JST.

3 Conclusions and unresolved questions

Within the tested configurations, PBT did not improve on standard ParT training beyond 1%, and with Training (8M) it was worse; the only gain found there came from combining separately trained models.

  • The explanations in phases 4–5 (decision horizon and overfitting; model diversity) are hypotheses, not tested directly.
  • All Training (8M) PBT results rest on one seed; the effect of the decision frequency (#6) is inconclusive; decisions within an epoch (#11) were never run.
  • Scope: one pretrained checkpoint, one optimizer, 50 epochs. Test (2.8M) was also inspected in diagnostics before November. No result was carried into a physics analysis.
