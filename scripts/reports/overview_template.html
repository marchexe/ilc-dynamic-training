Does Population Based Training give a better tagger?

Only a little, and only with little training data: PBT is 0.8% better than standard ParT training on 800k jets, but 2.7–12.4% worse on 8M jets. With 8M jets, the mean of two standard ParT models is 5.0% better.

With ten times more data, PBT loses

The best standard ParT model reaches 0.1611; PBT reaches 0.1812. Two fixes (judge LRs after a short cool-down, and add the final LR decay of standard ParT) reduce the loss to 2.7%, but PBT stays worse. The reason is shown in the next section.

With much data, an ensemble of two standard ParT models wins

The mean output of the two standard ParT models of one seed (start LRs 3×10−3 and 10−3) is better than the better one alone for all three seeds. Both models come from the LR scan that standard ParT runs anyway. Two PBT models do not help, because PBT copies weights between them. Distillation into one model gave no gain.

Why more data changes the answer

On 800k jets the model starts to memorise its training data after about 20 epochs (the training loss falls below the Validation loss), so lowering the LR early is right, and PBT finds that time. On 8M jets the model does not memorise, so a high LR for a long time and a decay at the end is best. PBT judges every LR change after two epochs, where a lower LR almost always looks better, so it lowers the LR too early and loses in the final decay (grey band).

Terms

Training: 800k = 2.4M jets, 8M = 24M jets. Validation (150k, then 1M jets) is checked after every epoch; it drives PBT and selects the model. Test (2.8M jets) is evaluated once per selected model. Mistag: mean background mistag rate at eight b- and c-tag working points.

Standard ParT: constant LR for 35 epochs, then ×0.01 over 15 epochs; five start LRs, the best is kept. PBT: five models; every 2 epochs (5 in September) the weakest copies the best one and changes its LR.

Limits

The 8M PBT trials have one seed. All trials start from one pretrained model. Test (2.8M) was also used in diagnostics before November. July–August was set-up with an older training contract; trials 5, 7, 8, 9, 11 and 30 are checks and are in the full trial list.
